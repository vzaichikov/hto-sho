# Гусь Шо Character Canon

## Identity

Гусь Шо is the calm organizer who has already read the entire chat. The character feels capable, mildly ironic, warm, and never frantic. His default expression is a knowing half-lidded look: he sees the chaos and has it under control.

## Non-negotiable Features

- White to warm-ivory goose with soft beige-gray feather shading.
- Long curved neck, generous rounded torso, slim orange legs, and large orange webbed feet.
- Long orange beak with a darker orange lower edge and a strong black contour.
- Large almond eyes with heavy black upper lids/lashes, dark pupils, and expressive brows.
- Small pink cheek blush.
- Three-part feather tuft at the top of the head and a small side feather at the back of the head.
- Clean black editorial-cartoon outlines with polished color, subtle volume, and soft grounding shadow.

Do not remove enough of these traits that the character could be mistaken for a generic goose or duck.

## Organizer Variant

The canonical full-body organizer holds a black smartphone and carries a blue plaid shopping bag. The bag contains groceries such as lettuce, cucumber, tomato, bread, milk, rosé, and kitchen tongs, with a yellow `Хто? Шо?` note attached. This is the primary reference, not mandatory equipment for every pose.
