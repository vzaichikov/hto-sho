# Views

## Keep customer UI goose-first, not technical
Customer-facing UI explains the outcome, current state, and next action in clear Ukrainian Goose voice. Do not expose jobs, tasks, OCR, caches, providers, internal IDs, versions, revisions, or schema terms. Keep those internally and present provenance as human-readable source material when useful.
