# Хто Шо? Brand System

## Brand Idea

`Хто Шо?` turns messy group-chat plans into a clear shopping list and a Сільпо cart. The emotional promise is relief: the service reads the chaos, remembers preferences, and leaves the group free to enjoy the event.

Core product loop:

1. The group shares screenshots or text from one or more chats.
2. The service understands attendees, diets, preferences, responsibilities, and changed decisions.
3. It produces one current list with sensible quantities.
4. It prepares the matching Сільпо cart for review.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream | `#FFF4DC` | Primary background and warm paper field |
| Red-orange | `#F24B35` | Main CTA, question mark, key emphasis |
| Green | `#4E9F62` | Positive state, underline, confirmation |
| Yellow | `#F7C84B` | Notes, highlights, playful accents |
| Ink | `#20201D` | Text, outlines, logo body |
| Beet | `#AB2C62` | Small expressive accent only |

Supporting neutrals may be derived from cream and ink for borders, muted text, and shadows. Do not replace the core red-orange with generic corporate blue or neon colors.

## Typography

- Use **Manrope** for interface text, body copy, labels, and readable headings.
- Use bold, clear, friendly headings with compact line-height.
- **Neucha** may be used as a display or handwritten accent in the current landing language. Keep it out of dense body copy and small UI controls.
- Hand-drawn underlines, arrows, hearts, speech bubbles, and paper notes should feel imperfect but intentional.

## Logo

The recognizable mark is stacked black `ХТО / ШО` lettering with a large red-orange question mark. Preserve its playful hand-drawn shape, compact stacking, and question mark. Do not recreate it as a plain typeset wordmark when the canonical artwork is available.

## Voice

- Friendly, direct, lightly mischievous, and competent.
- Use everyday Ukrainian and short sentences.
- The brand acknowledges chat chaos without blaming the group.
- Prefer concrete outcomes: `розгребемо чат`, `сформуємо список`, `додамо в кошик`, `ви — відпочивайте`.
- Keep Сільпо and OAuth claims precise. Never imply that a cart is final before the user reviews it.

## Visual Language

- Warm cream paper rather than pure white as the dominant field.
- Black hand-drawn outlines with bright, sparing accents.
- Rounded cards, speech bubbles, sticky notes, loose arrows, and small doodles.
- Product flows should visually progress from chat to understanding to list to basket.
- Photography and glossy corporate gradients are not part of the core language. Illustrations should remain warm, editorial, and slightly imperfect.

## UI Application

- Main CTA: red-orange fill, light text, rounded silhouette, simple directional arrow.
- Success/ready states: green; warnings or attention notes: yellow; destructive emphasis: red-orange with clear text.
- Use ink for body copy and structure. Muted text must remain readable against cream.
- Playful decoration must not obstruct controls, focus states, labels, or mobile layouts.
